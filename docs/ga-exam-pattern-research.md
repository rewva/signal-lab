# GA / GK exam-pattern research (SSC / Banking / Railways tier)

**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Method:** deep-research harness (5 search angles -> 21 sources fetched -> 89 claims ->
25 adversarially verified, 20 confirmed / 5 refuted). Feeds
`docs/specs/2026-06-10-gk-topic-selection-design.md` (topic weights + difficulty model).

---

## 1. Headline

Across the SSC / Banking / Railways tier, the GA/GK section is large and high-stakes, but its
composition differs sharply by exam family. **Current Affairs and General Science are
universally heavy; Banking/Financial Awareness is the major exam-specific block (IBPS/SBI
only); current-affairs recency clusters in a ~6-month window.**

---

## 2. Per-exam pattern (verified)

| Exam | GA/GK size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SSC CGL Tier 1 | **25 Q / 50 marks** (+2 / -0.50) | 1 of 4 equal sections; sectional timing. Broad academic spread, **no dedicated banking**. |
| SSC CGL Tier 2 | **25 Q / 75 marks** (3 marks each, -1) | Internal topic split NOT reliably establishable (refuted). |
| SSC CHSL | 25-Q section, **moderate difficulty** | Same broad spread as CGL. |
| RRB NTPC | **CBT1 40 Q / CBT2 50 Q** (1/3 negative) | The single largest section (40% of CBT1). CA > General Science > Static GK. |
| RRB Group D | **General Science 25 + GA/CA 20 = 45/100** (single CBT, 1/3 negative) | Science-heavy, **Class-10 CBSE level (basic)**. |
| SBI PO Mains | **60 Q / 60 marks / 45 min** | Dedicated "General/Economy/Banking Awareness" section (largest). |
| IBPS PO Mains | Dedicated GA section, **qualitative pattern only** | CA + Banking awareness dominate; exact Q/mark/time figures REFUTED -> treat as uncertain. |

Difficulty: SSC GA = easy-to-moderate; RRB Group D / General Science = Class-10 CBSE (basic);
NTPC CA = moderate; Banking PO Mains GA is the most demanding (regulatory/financial depth).

Current-affairs recency: ~**4-6 months** (banking), ~**6-8 months** (Railways).

## 3. Synthesis -- combined daily-quiz blueprint

### 3.1 Topic-weight blueprint (medium confidence; directional)

| Topic | Weight |
|---|---|
| Current Affairs (last ~6 months) | 30% |
| General Science | 18% |
| Static GK | 12% |
| History | 10% |
| Polity / Constitution | 8% |
| Geography | 8% |
| Economy | 7% |
| Banking / Financial Awareness | 5% |
| Sports / Awards / Misc | 2% |

### 3.2 Universal vs exam-specific

- **Universally heavy (all families):** Current Affairs, General Science. Static GK universal but lighter.
- **Core-academic (heavy SSC, present RRB, light Banking):** History, Polity, Geography, Economy.
- **Exam-specific:** Banking/Financial Awareness (RBI policy, fintech/UPI/CBDC, schemes,
  regulation) is a dedicated heavy block **only for IBPS/SBI**; General Science is
  disproportionately heavy for **Railways** (esp. Group D).

### 3.3 Difficulty distribution

~**50% Basic / 35% Intermediate / 15% Advanced** -- covers the entry-level Railways/CHSL
majority while still stretching CGL-Tier-2 and PO aspirants.

---

## 4. Caveats (important)

- **No exam body publishes an official topic-wise split** -- only gross section size
  (questions/marks) and a prose topic list are official. All per-topic weightages are
  coaching-industry estimates from past-paper recall (Testbook, PracticeMock, CareerPower, PW,
  Oliveboard, Adda247, Bankersadda). Read as directional ranges, not mandates.
- The two synthesis blueprints (topic % and 50/35/15 difficulty) are a reasoned aggregation,
  not independently sourced numbers -- tune to whichever exam family dominates the channel's
  actual audience.
- **Refuted in verification (dropped):** fixed "CA 12-15 + Banking 8-10" IBPS split (0-3);
  "IBPS PO Mains GA = 40 Q/40 marks/35 min" (1-2); "SSC CGL Tier 1 GA = 1 mark each" (0-3,
  correct is +2/-0.50). Exact IBPS-PO per-section numbers are the weakest part of the report.
- Figures reflect the 2024-2026 cycle (SSC CGL/CHSL 2024-26, RRB CEN 05/2024 NTPC + CEN 09/2025
  Group D, IBPS/SBI PO 2025 Mains); patterns shift each annual notification cycle.
