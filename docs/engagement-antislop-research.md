# Engagement & anti-slop research (short-form educational video, 2025-2026)

**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Method:** deep-research harness (6 angles -> 22 sources -> 95 claims -> 25 adversarially
verified, 19 confirmed / 6 refuted). Informs the differentiation layer of the daily-gk-quiz
design.

---

## 1. The headline (HIGH confidence, primary sources)

**The generic "single prompt -> A/B/C/D card + countdown" video is a textbook match for
YouTube's demonetizable "inauthentic content" category, and enforcement is channel-wide, not
per-video.** One templated pattern can demonetize the entire channel.

- YouTube's July 15, 2025 YPP update targets "mass-produced or repetitive content ... made with
  a template with little to no variation across videos, or ... easily replicable at scale,"
  explicitly naming "AI-generated content made with generic templates" and "image slideshows /
  scrolling text with minimal or no narrative, commentary, or educational value." Applies to
  the channel "as a whole."
- Meta ran a parallel crackdown (July 14, 2025, reaffirmed 2026): reused/unoriginal content
  "will lose access to monetization for a period AND receive reduced distribution on everything
  they share." ~500k accounts actioned H1 2025. -> cross-posting generic cards to Reels is
  risky too.

**The escape hatch (HIGH, consistent across both platforms):** AI is *fully* monetizable when
each video adds genuine original value and the core content visibly varies. YouTube CEO Neal
Mohan: "75% AI vs 5% AI" doesn't matter -- what matters is that it was done thoughtfully by a
human. **The dividing line is carelessness/low quality, NOT the amount of AI used.**

A consistent template is safe IF "the average viewer can clearly tell that content differs from
video to video." Branded intro/outro is fine; the *substance* must be materially varied with
added value (explanations, mnemonics, commentary) -- not just a swapped fact in an identical card.

---

## 2. Verified, actionable levers

| Lever | Confidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Material variation + per-video value, or channel-fatal | HIGH (primary) | Each video needs a real "why" / mnemonic / insight; the core must visibly differ |
| Retention > raw views | MEDIUM | Design for watch-through: hard hook in first 1-3s, tight pacing |
| Forced-choice + mild-disagreement CTAs beat generic ones | MEDIUM | Rotate "Which did you pick -- A or B?" and "Most people get this wrong -- disagree?" instead of "comment YES" |
| Edited captions, not raw automated AI captions | HIGH | Meta flags unedited auto-captions; cheap compliance win |

## 3. Refuted in verification -- do NOT treat as evidence-backed

These plausible tactics were killed by adversarial verification; they may still work but are
**untested hypotheses, not proven**:

- **Cliffhanger / unresolved-ending return-rate lift** (the "38-40% return" claim): REFUTED 0-3.
- **Daily-cadence / serialization as the primary return hook**: REFUTED 0-3.
- **Exam-specific segmentation as a proven differentiation wedge**: REFUTED 0-3. (Note: this does
  not undermine the separate "one combined channel" decision -- it just means segmentation isn't
  a demonstrated wedge to lean on.)
- **Single-token CTAs being algorithmically discounted** / **comment reply-length as a ranking
  signal**: REFUTED (1-2 / 0-3).

---

## 4. Largely UNANSWERED (open)

The core "which retention FORMAT beats a plain MCQ" question is not settled by surviving
evidence -- specific tactics (visual mnemonics, fill-in-the-blank, progressive difficulty,
polls, serialized hooks) lack verified support. CTA rotation *frequency* and audience-profiling
*cadence* were not quantified by any surviving claim. Treat any specific number (e.g.
"profile every 25 videos") as a guess to A/B test, not a finding.

## 5. How this maps to the operator's proven pipeline (see the credit-card study)

The verified mandate -- *genuine variation + visible added value + careful production* -- is
exactly what the operator's existing pipeline already does mechanically:

- **Block-composition rendering** (the credit-card block-resolver -> maps onto Remotion):
  genuine structural variation per video, produced systematically. The literal antidote to
  "templated sameness."
- **Field-trust verification gating** (verified/unverified labels; unverified never renders) ->
  an on-screen "verified -- <source>" badge = visible added value a slop farm cannot fake, and
  the OWN-DATA reliability moat made tangible.

**v1 decision (2026-06-10):** build the differentiation layer as *verified + varied +
pedagogical TEXT* -- block-composed structure, on-screen verified-source badge, a
mnemonic/"why it matters" per video, rotated forced-choice/disagreement CTAs, edited captions.
NO stock imagery yet (keeps the copyright-safe, automatable text-first decision); imagery can
layer in later via the proven image-sourcing+validation technique.
