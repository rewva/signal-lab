# Pakka — brand identity system

Master brand for a family of faceless Indian competitive-exam channels. "Pakka" (pakka/pukka)
= confirmed, solid, genuine, first-class — a pan-India word (Hindi-origin, fully absorbed into
Tamil/Telugu/Kannada slang and Indian English) that *means* the verified-source moat.

Channel #1 = **Pakka GK** (SSC + Banking + Railways general awareness, one combined channel).
Future verticals = Pakka Banking, Pakka SSC, Pakka IAS, Pakka NEET, Pakka Current Affairs.

## The idea: two motifs from the aspirant's own desk

1. **The OMR bubble.** Every MCQ exam is answered on an OMR sheet by filling one bubble. The
   filled bubble `(●)` is our mark of "the chosen, correct answer."
2. **The Pakka Seal.** Government certificates, admit cards and results carry an inked rubber
   stamp. Our seal is a small roundel `✓ PAKKA` set at a slight rotation like a real stamp.
   It is the **verification badge** that appears on every answer reveal next to the source — the
   brand literally stamps each fact "pakka."

These are specific to the subject, not generic edu-clip art. The seal is the signature element;
everything else stays quiet.

## Wordmark & lockup

`Pakka` is the constant master. The vertical (GK / Banking / IAS ...) sits in a stamped chip to
its right. The dot of one letter is a filled OMR bubble.

```
            filled OMR bubble as the accent dot
                     |
   P A K K  ●          ┌──────┐
   ----------------     │  GK  │   <- vertical chip (swaps per channel)
   P a k k a            └──────┘
   (display weight)     stamped, rotated -6deg
```

Master family extension (only the chip + its accent change):

```
Pakka [ GK ]      Pakka [ Banking ]      Pakka [ IAS ]      Pakka [ NEET ]
```

## Color

Dark-first (video reads better on deep ground; avoids the cream-serif / acid-green AI defaults).

| Token            | Hex       | Use                                           |
|------------------|-----------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Exam Ink         | `#15233B` | primary ground (deep government-ink blue)     |
| Ink Deep         | `#0E1828` | gradient floor / shadows                      |
| Paper            | `#EEF1F4` | on-ink text, cards                            |
| Stamp Vermilion  | `#FF5436` | master accent + the Pakka Seal ink            |
| Correct Green    | `#36C26E` | answer-reveal semantic (content, not brand)   |

Per-vertical accent (Ink + Seal stay constant so the family reads as one brand):
GK `#FF5436` (vermilion) · Banking `#2BB6A8` (teal) · SSC `#6C5CE7` (indigo) ·
IAS `#C0392B` (maroon) · NEET `#2E9E5B` (green).

## Type

- **Display + body: Anek** (Latin + Devanagari) — a modern, variable, multi-script Indian
  typeface. It is purpose-built for pan-India scripts, so the same system carries Hinglish and a
  future Hindi cut without a face swap. Non-generic and subject-aligned.
- **Numerals/utility: Anek tabular** (day number, OMR letters A/B/C/D) for steady alignment.
- Display used heavy and with restraint; body lighter. The type is part of the identity, not a
  neutral carrier.

## The one risk

The rotated, ink-textured **Pakka Seal** stamped onto every verified answer — physical and a
little irreverent in a category of flat digital slop, and it pays off the brand word and the moat
in a single mark. Spend the boldness there; keep the rest disciplined.

## Voice

Plain, confident, aspirant-to-aspirant Hinglish where it helps. Tagline options:
*"Verified GK. Every day."* (EN) / *"Har sawaal ka pakka jawaab."* (Hinglish).
