# Idea bank

Running list of vetted ideas to draw test bets from. Every entry must pass the **moat filter** (ACT / WATCH / OWN-DATA / SHIP) and the **feasibility gate** (no walled-garden scraping) from `strategy.md`.

## The shape that wins

The strongest tools here are **not generators** (those are wrappers). They are **agents that act, watchers that alert, or fresh-data engines** — things a chatbot structurally can't replace.

## Chatbot-proof candidates (buildable, no walled-garden scraping)

### WATCH (monitor 24/7, alert instantly)
1. **Job first-alert + auto-match** — pings the second a matching role posts (Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby have public endpoints). Subscription. Postable.
2. **Grant & scholarship matcher** — watches public funding/scholarship databases. Students, researchers, nonprofits pay. Uncrowded.
3. **Regulation / policy-change watcher** — monitors govt/regulator feeds, alerts businesses to relevant changes. B2B.
4. **New-business / permit lead engine** *(top pick)* — public business-registration/permit records -> fresh leads for contractors, realtors, agencies. B2B, real money, low competition.
5. **Competitor change monitor** — tracks a rival's public site/prices/job posts (hiring spree = expansion signal). B2B.
6. **Restock / drop alerts** — retailer stock APIs for hard-to-get items. Subscription.
7. **App-store review & rank watcher** — alerts app makers to review/ranking shifts. B2B.
8. **Price-drop + best-time-to-buy predictor** — multi-store tracking + AI timing. Affiliate + premium.

### ACT (do tasks on the user's own accounts)
9. **Auto-apply job agent** — tailors + submits applications. "Applied to 150 jobs while I slept" — viral.
10. **Refund / chargeback auto-filer** *(top pick)* — finds owed refunds and files them. "Free money" = wildly postable.
11. **Price-protection claimer** — watches price after purchase, auto-files refund on drops.
12. **Flight-delay compensation agent** — watches flights, auto-files EU261/comp claims.
13. **Warranty vault + auto-claim** — tracks purchases, files warranty claims when things break.
14. **Subscription canceller / bill negotiator** — kills hidden charges, negotiates bills. Cut of savings.
15. **Auto cross-poster** — generates AND actually publishes scheduled content to socials.
16. **Data-removal agent** — continuously scrubs the user from data brokers. Proven money (Incogni), but crowded.

### OWN-DATA (fresh, verified dataset chatbots lack)
17. **"What changed today" live digest** — fresh papers / tools / funding in a niche. Subscription.
18. **Live outage / status aggregator** — real-time "is X down" for a set of services. B2B alerts.
19. **Always-fresh verified directory** — the operator's verification superpower pointed at a non-finance niche where every other listing is stale. Leads/affiliate.

### SHIP (physical)
20. **On-demand custom product agent** — design -> manufacture -> ship (custom books, engraved goods). Product margin.

### Content-funnel candidate
- **GK / competitive-exam** — daily question video (distribution) -> verified always-fresh question bank (OWN-DATA money + moat). Locked as a test bet; see `plan.md`.

## Top picks (money x feasibility x viral x uncrowded)
- **#10 refund auto-filer** and the "free money" cluster (#11-13) — most postable.
- **#4 new-business lead engine** — best B2B money, low competition.
- **#1 job first-alert** — very feasible (public ATS endpoints).

## Graveyard (rejected — see strategy.md decision log)
- Carousel/content generators (crowded wrappers).
- Verdict-machine / scam-scanner (didn't land).
- Flip-finder / resale arbitrage (failed feasibility gate: FB Marketplace/eBay block scraping).
- B2B data API, widgets, finance verticals (already WhichWise's roadmap, out of scope).
